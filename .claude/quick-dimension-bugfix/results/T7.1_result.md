### Tool behavior worth recording

`manage_adr(mode="update")` **replaces** the entire ADR store; it does not append. The first
update in this mission wrote ADR-2026-08-04B alone and silently dropped ADR-2026-08-04A. That was
only the visible last overwrite, not the full scope of the store loss.

Later forensic recovery proved a larger history: all 18 recorded `update` calls submitted entry-only
payloads, never the full store. By the first retained `mode="get"` on 2026-07-19, the four
2026-07-12-era entries and all six prose sections were already gone from the live store, and the
seven later transcript-only entries (`ADR-2026-07-17B`, `07-17C`, `07-18A`, `07-19A`, `07-20A`,
`07-22A`, `07-30A`) were then destroyed in later waves by the same full-replace behavior. Total
loss: 11 items. All 11 were restored verbatim on 2026-08-05 from git plus Claude session
transcripts. Root cause, full timeline, and per-entry provenance now live in
`.Dossier/ADR Store Loss - Root Cause and Recovery Inventory.md`; the transcript-recovered entries
also have a verbatim archive in `.Dossier/ADR Store Loss - Verbatim Recovery Archive.md`.
