## Objective
Freeze the package vocabulary for runtime verdicts so later evidence-analysis tasks classify cases consistently.

## Do
1. Define `Supported`, `Unsupported-by-design`, and `Defect` in compact operational terms.
2. Identify the exact signals each label depends on:
   - dimension created or not,
   - segment-value correctness,
   - audit gate truth values,
   - honesty of dialog/diagnostic output,
   - crash/no-crash behavior.
3. Clarify where `Exact` and complete `Reversed` fit.
4. Clarify that missing evidence is not itself a defect verdict; it is a `BLOCKED` orchestration state.

## PASS only if
- The three labels are mutually exclusive.
- The labels are usable by `T2.4`, `T3.5`–`T3.8`, `T4.4`, and `T6.7` without reinterpretation.
- The result preserves the strict audit policy.

## BLOCKED if
- The contract does not provide enough information to make the labels operational.

## Downstream needs
- All later evidence-judging tasks use these exact verdict terms.
