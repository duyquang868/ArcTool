## Objective
Judge the Grid safe-failure evidence variant by variant.

## Do
1. For each Grid variant, compare the observed outcome to the predicted acceptable safe-failure mode.
2. State whether the variant outcome is Unsupported-by-design or Defect.
3. Call out any accidental dimension creation, misleading success, or crash as Defect.
4. Keep judgments variant-specific; do not merge them prematurely.

## PASS only if
- Every supplied variant receives an explicit verdict.
- The result distinguishes honest no-support behavior from silent wrong behavior.
- It does not treat missing evidence as a defect.

## BLOCKED if
- The EV-3 excerpt omits a variant outcome or the necessary visible evidence.
