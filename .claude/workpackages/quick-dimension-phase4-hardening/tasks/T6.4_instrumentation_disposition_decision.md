## Objective
Decide whether the Phase 5 timing instrumentation stays in the final code or is removed before closure.

## Do
1. Decide one of exactly two outcomes:
   - `KEEP` — instrumentation remains as durable support diagnostics, or
   - `REMOVE` — instrumentation was temporary and should be stripped before closure.
2. Justify the decision using:
   - the Phase 5 performance value it delivered,
   - the EV-6 regression result,
   - the maintenance cost of keeping it.
3. If `KEEP`, state why the permanent log output is worth the extra code.
4. If `REMOVE`, state why the evidence mission is complete without permanent timings.
5. Name the exact code action `T6.5` must take.

## PASS only if
- The decision is unambiguous: `KEEP` or `REMOVE`.
- The result gives `T6.5` an exact instruction.
- The decision does not reopen collector logic or audit semantics.

## NO_GO if
- Regression is not cleared well enough to trust either keeping or removing the instrumentation.

## Downstream needs
- `T6.5` needs the exact disposition action and the rationale.
